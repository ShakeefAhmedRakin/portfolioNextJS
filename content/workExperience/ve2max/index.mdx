- Improved reliability and data integrity through **database schema hardening, migrations, and access-control enforcement** across ClubOS's core entities.
- Identified and resolved critical **production and post-merge regressions**, maintaining design and user-experience consistency across ClubOS.
- Provide ongoing **development and maintenance** to keep ClubOS stable, secure, and production-ready.
